Why import cars from the USA?

Buying a car in America is several times more profitable, no matter how paradoxical it may sound. This is due to thoughtful logistics, the level of development of services for assessing the condition of a car and the process of buying a car itself.

Most US citizens lease a car for several years and during the entire period of operation the leasing company itself is engaged in constant maintenance of the car, as a result of which cars from the USA are one of the best choices.

Why such a low price?

Damaged cars from the USA are bought from insurance company auctions. At these auctions, the car loses half its price even due to minimal damage. If we take into account the monetary costs, namely redemption, delivery, customs and repairs, then the price of a car will be 35-50% higher, and new ones will cost astronomical money.
